Cashback

Cashback is paid by the cashback network, not by this extension. The template hands the visit to your network so the purchase is attributed to the user's account.

Cashback payouts

Virtual Visa cards are issued through your proxy to Tango Card's order API. Enter your production platform credentials below, or leave them on the proxy; either way the extension keeps only the reference and redemption link it gets back — never a full card number. Without a connected proxy there is nothing to issue, so cash out stays unavailable.

Sponsored offers

Shoppers can earn a small amount for visiting an advertiser, paid into the same cashback wallet. Point this at a sponsor or ad programme you are entitled to use: the extension shows only the offers it returns and records only the amount it books for a visit. Incentivising clicks on ordinary display ads breaks most ad networks' terms — use a programme that pays for these visits itself.

GET returns the offers; POST with {"offerId": "…"} books a visit. See the README for both shapes. Blank hides the Earn extra block.

Sent only to the endpoint above, and never exported.

Price history

Optional. Local price tracking works without a key: every product page you visit records the price you saw, on this device, and the panel reports the lowest one it has seen.
