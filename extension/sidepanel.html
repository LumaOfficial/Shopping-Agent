    Luma Pay Shopping Agent

    What the panel asks your browser for, and what it does with it — before it does anything for you.

    • One purpose. Finding codes, cashback and prices while you shop, and filling only the store's own promo field.
    • Why it asks to see sites. It has to recognize the shop you opened, read the codes and prices that page publishes, and hide the ad slots it blocks. Nothing else on the page is read.
    • How it is paid for. Affiliate cashback, labeled sponsored offers, and the ad value it credits to your wallet when ad blocking is on.
    • What stays here. Account, codes, prices and balances live in this browser profile. Card numbers are never read.

              Good to know

              Where do the codes come from?

              Most come from the page you are on — banners, the cart and the store's deals pages. A feed you connect in Settings is merged in and labelled by source.

              Does Luma Pay see my card details?

              No. Card numbers, expiry dates and security codes are never read, stored or sent. Your browser's own autofill fills them.

              When does cashback become cash?

              A tracked visit stays pending until the store's return window passes (30 days), then it becomes available to cash out.

              How is the price low worked out?

              From prices recorded on product pages you visit on this device, plus an optional 90-day low from a product API in Settings.
